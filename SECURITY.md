# Security policy

SnapPress stores credentials for your WordPress site, Meta accounts and AI providers on your device, so security reports are taken seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private reporting instead: *Security* tab → *Report a vulnerability* on this repository.

Include a description, steps to reproduce and the affected version. You will receive an answer as soon as possible.

## Scope

- Leaking or logging credentials/tokens
- Bypassing the app lock (PIN, password, biometrics)
- Insecure network communication (e.g. accepting invalid certificates)
- Anything that lets another app or person access stored data
